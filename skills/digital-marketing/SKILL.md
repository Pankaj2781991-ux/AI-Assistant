# Digital Marketing

Use this skill for campaign planning and marketing execution support. The goal is to produce assets a marketer can actually reuse, not vague promotional advice.

## Workflow

1. Identify the business, offer, audience, and desired conversion.
2. If a website is visible, extract a lightweight marketing profile first.
3. Choose channels based on the request:
   - `Google` for search intent, SEO, and high-intent acquisition
   - `Meta` for awareness, retargeting, and social conversion
   - `LinkedIn` for B2B and professional audience messaging
4. Produce structured outputs before browser clicking:
   - positioning
   - audience segments
   - keyword clusters
   - messaging angles/hooks
   - channel-specific asset packs
   - landing-page checklist
5. If the user wants campaign setup, prepare the campaign plan first and stop before risky final publish/confirm actions unless explicit approval is given.

## Rules

- Prefer reusable briefs and copy packs over one-off prose.
- Keep claims grounded in visible website or user-provided context.
- Default to one core offer and one primary conversion action.
- Use concise, direct language instead of brand fluff.
- For paid ads, favor clear benefit + proof + CTA structure.
- For landing pages, focus on headline clarity, offer clarity, proof, objections, and CTA.

## Outputs

- `campaign brief`: positioning, funnel stage, conversion goal, audience, keyword clusters, messaging angles
- `channel packs`: Google, Meta, LinkedIn variants
- `campaign plan`: settings-ready plan for Meta or Google Ads
- `landing-page checklist`: headline, offer, proof, objections, CTA, form friction
