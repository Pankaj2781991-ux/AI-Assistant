## Messaging Angle Patterns

- Benefit first: `Get <outcome> without <pain>`
- Pain to solution: `Still dealing with <pain>? <brand> helps you <outcome>`
- Proof-led: `<number/customer/proof> teams use <brand> to <outcome>`
- CTA-led: `See pricing`, `Book demo`, `Start free`, `Get quote`
